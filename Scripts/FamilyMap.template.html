INTERACTIVE GUIDE · NOT THE NATIVE APP

Group buttons open a page. Action buttons here only describe what the native app does.
Back moves up one level; Home reaches all five families. In the native app, a new screenshot can temporarily open the viewer and Back returns to the interrupted page. × hides the native bar and pauses collection—not the same action as Back.
This guide does not access your clipboard, conversations, Mac, permissions, or files. It makes no network requests. Native build and hardware behavior require testing on your Mac.
